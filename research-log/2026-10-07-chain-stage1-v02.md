## What landed (compiler v0.2 stage 1)

- G1 (rule atom at non-terminal row) lifted **only** for a single
  unit body whose literal is (row-1 via with the relay intact) or
  (the adjacent-below derived atom). The intermediate row reuses the
  terminal unit pair verbatim; its reader relays the atom's
  truth-typed `{a}-t-done` glue up the V column — the chain link.
- Unit conduits read `below_d` (the D-column northward glue the row
  below actually exposes) instead of v0.1's `below_done`; for every
  fact row below these are the same glue, so all v0.1 builds are
  byte-identical (probed: PC1-PC8 counts unchanged).
- Fact/false rows above a derived row relay the V-column chain glue
  instead of the row-1 via (`below_v`), so a middle-fact chain
  stays wired; a row-1 via read above a derived row now refuses
  ("suspended") — the via is consumed by the chain link.
- New refusals (PR3 split realized): non-adjacent chain reads,
  OR at an intermediate row (each variant conduit exposes its own
  north glue — no truth-OR relay), AND at an intermediate row,
  chain depth > 2 (designs/008 §6), AND conduit over a derived
  hi (would read its variant glue, not a truth-typed value).
