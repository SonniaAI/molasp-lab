## Anchors (search-snippet level — see honesty box)

1. **Seed provenance is a first-class kinetic variable.** Rothemund,
   Papadakis & Winfree 2004 (Algorithmic Self-Assembly of DNA Sierpinski
   Triangles, PLOS Biology 2(12): e424, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.0020424):
   seeded growth is the observed route to the algorithmic pattern — the
   macro-scale existence proof that what nucleates determines what grows.
   Verified this tick at snippet level (PMC534809).
2. **Nucleation barriers are programmable per nucleus.** Schulman &
   Winfree 2007 (crystals with a programmable kinetic barrier to
   nucleation): barrier height is a designed function of the nucleus —
   kinetics can differ by construction between seed neighborhoods.
   URL fetched in the sibling note (same day).
3. **kTAM's exchangeability premise.** Winfree 1998 kTAM as restated in
   Patitz' 2013 review: constant monomer concentration, no
   provenance term. Under the premise, terminal-pair windows are
   exchangeable ACROSS blocks — account A is "the premise holds at
   census scale"; account B is a candidate violation.
4. **Concentration heterogeneity stratifies outcomes.** Chen & Kao
   (arXiv:1210.5786): tile-type concentrations set error and
   completion-time scaling — one mechanism by which block-local
   composition differences could print persistent census differences.
5. **Depletion couples assemblies through the shared pool.** Evans &
   Winfree 2017 and the DNA31 tile-blocker note treat depletion as a
   physical principle to engineer around. The paired same-architecture
   design holds substrate and conditions fixed, so a persistent GAP-vs-A6
   contrast is attributable to block provenance rather than pool drift.
